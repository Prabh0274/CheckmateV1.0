Checkmate — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Checkmate is a to-do and reminders app for Windows. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information. The short version: your tasks stay on your own devices, and the developer does not collect, see, or receive any of your personal data.

What Checkmate stores, and where

The tasks, lists, notes, and settings you create are stored locally on your own PC, in your user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Checkmate). This information is never sent to the developer and is not uploaded to any server operated by the developer.

Sync (optional)

If you turn on Sync, Checkmate saves a single data file into a cloud folder that you choose and control — such as your own iCloud Drive or OneDrive — so your other devices can read it. This uses your own cloud account. The developer has no access to that folder, that account, or its contents. If you do not turn on Sync, nothing leaves your PC.

Reminders and notifications

Checkmate uses Windows notifications to show reminders. These are handled entirely by Windows on your device. No reminder content is sent to the developer.

Advertising (free version)

The free version of Checkmate shows a small in-app promotional panel. By default this displays the developer's own tips and offers and does not use the internet to load outside ads, and does not collect information about you. If a third-party ad source is ever enabled in a future version, this policy will be updated to describe it, and any such ads would be subject to that provider's own privacy policy. Purchasing "Checkmate Pro" removes the panel.

Purchases

"Checkmate Pro" is sold through the Microsoft Store. Payments and licensing are handled by Microsoft, not by the developer. The developer does not receive your payment details. Microsoft's handling of that transaction is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Windows Hello lock (optional)

If you enable the app lock, Checkmate asks Windows Hello to confirm it's you. This check is performed by Windows. Checkmate never sees your PIN, face, or fingerprint data.

Data you export

You can export your data to a JSON or CSV file. Where that file then goes is entirely up to you.

Children

Checkmate is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.
